VaultBasis Edge

Design Partner Preview — Quick Start

CONFIDENTIAL · DESIGN PARTNER ONLY

1 Download

Open the download link you received by email. Save the file to your Downloads folder.

The download contains:

  • VaultBasis application
  • This Quick Start guide

2 Install

Current preview (RC2): Open your Downloads folder. You will see the VaultBasis application file and a launcher called launch_vaultbasis.command in the same folder. Keep them together. Double-click the launcher to start. If macOS blocks it, see the Troubleshooting guide — Section A — for the one-time approval for your macOS version.

Target experience (RC3 forward): Double-click the VaultBasis installer, follow the on-screen instructions, then open VaultBasis from Applications. The VaultBasis workspace opens automatically. No Terminal steps, no addresses to type.

3 Open VaultBasis

Current preview (RC2 workaround): Double-click launch_vaultbasis.command. Leave the small black window open — closing it stops VaultBasis.

Then open Chrome, Safari, or Edge yourself and go to:

http://127.0.0.1:8000
What happens next: Your VaultBasis dashboard appears in the browser. Manual open at the address above is a temporary RC2 workaround only — RC3 target is automatic workspace open with no address to type.

4 Start with the sample case

Use the included sample data first to become familiar with reconciliation, findings, and the Outcome Receipt before using real case information.

  1. Open the sample case from the dashboard
  2. Run the comparison — Click "Run Deterministic Reconciliation"
  3. Review results — See what matches, what differs, and what cannot be resolved
  4. Download the Evidence Bundle — A signed receipt (.zip) you can share with your reviewer

Your information stays local during the supported Edge reconciliation workflow. See the MMP-1 Local Processing Test Summary for the exact tested boundary.

How It Works

VaultBasis processes your files on your own computer during the reconciliation workflow — your browser talks to VaultBasis at a local address on your machine, not to a cloud service. You control the signed receipt and decide who sees it. It records what was compared, what matched, what differed, and what was left unresolved. It does not determine tax or legal compliance — your reviewer does.

The receipt can be independently verified by anyone — without contacting VaultBasis — using the included offline verifier tool.
