Welcome to VaultBasis Edge

VaultBasis independently compares supported broker information with supported tax-ledger information and shows what agrees, what differs, and what remains unresolved.

1 Install VaultBasis

Open the VaultBasis installer you downloaded and follow the onscreen instructions.

macOS: open the installer, then open VaultBasis from Applications.

Windows: open the installer, then open VaultBasis from the Start menu.

2 Open VaultBasis

VaultBasis opens your local workspace automatically.

3 Try the Sample Case

Start with the included sample case to see how VaultBasis identifies agreements, differences, and unresolved information.

4 Review your Outcome Receipt

When reconciliation is complete, VaultBasis can produce a signed Outcome Receipt. Its payload integrity and signature can be independently verified.

Need help?

Open the Troubleshooting Guide — installation and startup help for your operating system. If VaultBasis couldn't start, your files have not been changed.

Important limitation: VaultBasis performs bounded deterministic reconciliation of supported information under declared semantics. It does not determine tax correctness, legal compliance, or whether source information is complete or factually accurate.
