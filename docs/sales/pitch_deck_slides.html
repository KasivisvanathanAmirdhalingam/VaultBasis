Market Catalyst & Macro Problem

The Form 1099-DA Basis Blindspot

Why the IRS rollout accelerates CPA review backlogs rather than fixing them

1. Gross Proceeds Only

Brokers and crypto exchanges report sales proceeds on Form 1099-DA, but omit acquisition dates and cost basis for non-covered wallets and off-exchange transfers.

2. Strict Legal Burden

Treasury regulations mandate that taxpayers and CPAs must substantiate cost basis. Filing without reconciliation risks massive client tax overpayments or audit penalties.

3. Calculation Chaos

Broker reports and taxpayer software ledgers produce divergent gains, rounding errors, and unverified numbers with zero standardized workpaper evidence.

Source: IRS Notice 2024-56 & Rev. Proc. 2024-28 (TD 10000) Slide 02 / 12
