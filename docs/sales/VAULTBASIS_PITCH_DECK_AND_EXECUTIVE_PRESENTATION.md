## Slide 2: The Macro Catalyst — IRS Form 1099-DA & The Basis Blindspot

### Slide Content:
- **The Regulatory Tsunami:** Starting Tax Year 2025/2026, IRS Form 1099-DA mandates gross proceeds reporting across crypto exchanges, brokers, and hosted wallets.
- **The Critical Flaw:** The IRS itself explicitly warns that many 1099-DA statements **will not include cost basis** (non-covered transactions, decentralized protocols, self-custody transfers, unlinked exchange accounts).
- **The Legal Burden:** Taxpayers and CPAs bear strict legal responsibility under Treasury regulations to establish and substantiate cost basis ([IRS Digital Asset Guidance](https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets)).
- **The Result:** CPAs face massive reconciliation backlogs, divergent calculations between broker reports and client ledgers, and zero standardized workpaper evidence.

### Presenter Notes:
- "The IRS didn't solve crypto tax reporting with Form 1099-DA—they accelerated the crisis. Brokers report sales proceeds, but not what the taxpayer paid for the asset. If a CPA files without reconciling, their client faces massive over-taxation or audit penalties."
