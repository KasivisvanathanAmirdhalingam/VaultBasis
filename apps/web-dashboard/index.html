Practitioner Guide & Knowledge Base

Guidance for digital asset evidence assurance in VaultBasis Edge.

What is a VaultBasis Case?

A Case is a bounded reconciliation activity organizing a client engagement for a specific tax year. It evaluates declared Broker Evidence against Tax-Ledger Evidence under fixed deterministic rules.

What are the Evidence Sources?

Source A (Broker Evidence): Form 1099-DA CSV/PDF from exchange/broker.
Source B (Tax Ledger): Koinly or accounting system CSV export.

Understanding Outcome States

  • AGREED / MATCHED: Supported compared information agrees under declared rules.
  • MATERIAL DIFFERENCE: A declared variance exists (e.g. Basis or Proceeds difference) requiring review.
  • UNRESOLVED: Evidence is insufficient to determine agreement.
