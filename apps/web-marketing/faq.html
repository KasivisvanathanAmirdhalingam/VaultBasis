Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Answers to common questions about VaultBasis, how reconciliation works, the Outcome Receipt, and the Design-Partner Preview.

Understanding VaultBasis

What is VaultBasis?

VaultBasis is an independent evidence-assurance system that deterministically reconciles supported digital-asset broker and tax-ledger information. It identifies agreements, differences, and unresolved information, and produces a signed Outcome Receipt that can be independently verified.

What problem does it solve?

Tax software and brokers can produce different gain/loss or cost-basis figures for the same transactions. VaultBasis independently compares both, identifies where they agree or differ, and records the outcome in a verifiable signed receipt — without deciding which source is legally correct.

Who is VaultBasis intended for?

Tax practitioners, enrolled agents, CPAs, reviewers, and enterprise evaluators who need an independent comparison of supported digital-asset information sources and a verifiable record of that comparison.

Is VaultBasis tax preparation software?

No. VaultBasis compares information. It does not prepare tax returns, calculate tax liability, provide tax advice, or determine correct tax treatment.

Does VaultBasis decide which source is correct?

No. VaultBasis identifies agreements, differences, and unresolved information. Professional judgment about which source is accurate and what the correct tax treatment is remains the practitioner's responsibility.

What does "independent reconciliation" mean?

VaultBasis is deliberately separate from both the broker and the tax-ledger system. It applies fixed, published comparison rules to the information both sources provide, without a stake in either outcome.

What information sources are currently supported?

The current Design-Partner Preview supports Form 1099-DA (U.S. digital asset broker reporting) and Koinly Capital Gains CSV. See Scope & Limitations for the exact supported format specifications.

What happens when information is missing?

Missing basis, dates, prices, or other information is preserved as Unresolved. VaultBasis never substitutes a default value, zero, or assumption for missing information.

Outcomes and Findings

What does a matched outcome mean?

The supported information from both sources agrees under the declared reconciliation rules.

What is a declared difference?

The two sources report different values for the same item — for example different cost basis figures for the same asset. VaultBasis records the exact difference without determining which source is correct.

What does Unresolved mean?

Required information is missing from one or both sources. The reconciliation cannot reach a definite agreement or difference conclusion for that item.

Why doesn't VaultBasis automatically resolve missing information?

Because assuming a value would introduce an error that is not visible in the Outcome Receipt. Preserving Unresolved keeps the evidence honest and flags the item for practitioner review.

What information does provenance provide?

Each item in an Outcome Receipt references the source rows and file hashes it came from, so the receipt can be traced back to its source evidence.

What should a practitioner do after a difference is identified?

Review the original source documents to understand why the sources differ. Apply professional judgment to determine the correct treatment. VaultBasis provides the comparison; the practitioner determines the appropriate response.

Outcome Receipt

What is an Outcome Receipt?

A digitally signed JSON document recording the outcome of a VaultBasis reconciliation. It contains the case summary, differences, provenance references, and a cryptographic signature.

Why is an Outcome Receipt signed?

The signature allows any reviewer to verify that the receipt has not been changed since it was created and that the signature matches the key declared in the receipt.

Can another person verify it?

Yes. The independent Web Verifier at /verifier verifies any supported Outcome Receipt in a browser without a VaultBasis account. The specification and test vectors are public.

What does successful verification establish?

That the receipt conforms to the declared evidence contract, that its cryptographic signature is valid for the key declared in the receipt, and that the receipt has not been altered since signing.

What does successful verification NOT establish?

It does not determine tax correctness, source accuracy or completeness, legal compliance, or professional judgment. It does not independently authenticate the originating VaultBasis installation. It does not constitute government approval or IRS endorsement.

Does a valid signature prove the tax treatment is correct?

No. The signature proves the receipt has not been altered. Tax correctness is not determined by VaultBasis and is shown as NOT DETERMINED in the verifier.

What happens if a receipt is modified?

Signature verification will fail. The verifier will report FAIL (TAMPERED / INVALID).

Privacy and Security

Where is source evidence processed?

Source evidence used in reconciliation is processed within the declared local application boundary on your device. See Scope & Limitations for tested network behaviour and known limitations.

Does the Web Verifier upload my receipt?

No. Verification runs entirely in your browser using WebCrypto. Receipt contents are not sent to VaultBasis.

Where is the signing key stored?

The Ed25519 signing key is generated locally within the VaultBasis Edge application. See Scope & Limitations for the declared local application boundary and known limitations.

What should I send when requesting support?

Describe the issue and the steps to reproduce it. Do not send taxpayer CSV files, receipts containing real taxpayer data, or other sensitive evidence in support correspondence.

How do I report a security concern?

Use the security disclosure route at /security-disclosure.

Design-Partner Preview

What does Design-Partner Preview mean?

VaultBasis is currently available to invited participants for evaluation using supported sample or test data. This is a controlled evaluation release, not a production service. Provisioning is not automatic.

How do I get access?

Use the Request Design-Partner Access form on the home page. Access is reviewed and provisioned individually.
