How It Works

Four steps from supported client evidence to a signed, independently verifiable Evidence Receipt.

1

Provide Supported Evidence

Select supported broker Form 1099-DA and tax-ledger records within the declared scope.

2

Reconcile Locally

VaultBasis applies declared deterministic reconciliation rules to compare the two sources on your computer.

3

Review the Outcome

Review agreements, differences, and unresolved items with clear explanation and record provenance. Unresolved facts are never silently resolved.

4

Produce an Evidence Receipt

Generate a signed portable Evidence Receipt whose integrity can be independently verified without the VaultBasis runtime.

Evaluation Scope

Canonical Evaluation Boundary

VaultBasis enforces an unmistakable boundary between deterministic machine reconciliation and the filing practitioner's professional tax judgment.

✓ WHAT VAULTBASIS DETERMINES
✗ WHAT VAULTBASIS DOES NOT DETERMINE

Source Agreement

Whether supported information from two declared sources agrees under the declared reconciliation semantics.

Tax Correctness

VaultBasis does not determine whether a taxpayer's tax position is legally correct, optimal, or acceptable to the IRS.

Declared Differences

Surfaces exact observed differences in proceeds, cost basis, or dates without deciding which source is correct.

Legal Compliance

VaultBasis does not determine compliance with federal, state, or other applicable tax law.

Unresolved Information

Flags missing facts without inventing or substituting default dates, zero values, or arbitrary pricing assumptions.

Source Truth

A successful comparison does not establish that either source file is authentic, complete, or factually accurate.

Payload Integrity & Signature

Verifies that an Evidence Receipt has not been changed since it was signed, and confirms the signature is valid for the key that produced it.

Professional Judgment

VaultBasis does not replace the practitioner's professional judgment, due diligence, or responsibility for the resulting tax position or filing.

Deployment Boundary

Local-First Evidence Processing

VaultBasis is designed for local evidence processing. Client files used in reconciliation are processed within the declared local application boundary on your computer. Website, license request, and distribution interactions are separate from local processing.
