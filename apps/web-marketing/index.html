Filing Season Context

Why Form 1099-DA Changes Digital-Asset Reconciliation

Beginning with tax year 2025 returns, brokers report digital asset transactions on Form 1099-DA. Tax professionals now face a structured reconciliation workload.

1. Proceeds Reported First

Brokers Report Gross Proceeds

Applicable digital-asset brokers began reporting gross proceeds for 2025 transactions, creating an official IRS reporting record for client activity.

2. Basis Still In Client Ledger

Most Statements Omit Basis

For 2025 transactions, most broker Form 1099-DA statements will not report cost basis. Practitioners must substantiate basis from client records.

3. Defensible Review Trail

Document Findings

The IRS advises tax professionals to review and reconcile transactions across exchanges, wallets and accounts, apply appropriate cost-basis methods, and document their findings.

Positioning & Scope

VaultBasis Complements Your Existing Tax Software

VaultBasis sits between raw transaction sources and tax preparation—reconciling source records so you can file with confidence.

✓ WHAT VAULTBASIS DOES

  • ✓ Deterministic Source Comparison: Identifies exact matches and variances across broker reports and tax ledgers.
  • ✓ Unresolved Stays Visible: Missing cost basis, unknown dates, or unlinked lots remain explicitly flagged.
  • ✓ Evidence Receipts: Produces cryptographic Ed25519 receipts verifiable by reviewers without VaultBasis software.
  • ✓ Local Case Processing: VaultBasis Edge processes supported client reconciliation and evidence locally. Client case files are not required to be uploaded to VaultBasis cloud services for normal Edge case processing.

✗ WHAT VAULTBASIS DOES NOT DO

  • ✗ Does not replace tax software: Use UltraTax, Drake, CCH, or Lacerte for return preparation and filing.
  • ✗ Does not give tax opinions: Does not determine whether a tax position is legally correct or optimal.
  • ✗ Does not invent missing facts: Never silently resolves gaps with default zero prices or artificial dates.
  • ✗ Does not replace judgment: The CPA or EA remains responsible for professional due diligence and tax filing.
