About VaultBasis

Digital-Asset Tax Reconciliation for Tax Professionals

VaultBasis is digital-asset tax reconciliation software for tax professionals. It helps practitioners compare supported broker reporting with client tax-ledger records, identify agreements, differences and unresolved information, and preserve a verifiable record of the reconciliation.

What VaultBasis Does

VaultBasis provides deterministic, local reconciliation between broker-reported Form 1099-DA evidence and client crypto tax-ledger records. It helps CPAs, EAs, and tax professionals review digital-asset engagements efficiently, separate exact matches from discrepancies that need professional review, and produce cryptographically signed Evidence Receipts.

Why It Exists

Tax software and broker reporting systems each produce information used in the tax workflow. The system producing a tax-ledger outcome is not independent of that outcome. VaultBasis provides a separate deterministic comparison against supported broker information under declared rules, without deciding which source is legally or factually correct.

When source data contains gaps or missing dates, VaultBasis preserves them as unresolved facts rather than inventing assumptions. The result is a signed Evidence Receipt that records exactly what the comparison found.

What VaultBasis Is Not

VaultBasis is not tax software, a tax advisor, or a compliance service. It does not:

  • Determine whether a tax position is legally correct or acceptable to the IRS
  • Establish legal compliance with federal, state, or other applicable tax law
  • Determine whether source information is authentic, complete, or accurate
  • Replace the practitioner's professional judgment or responsibility
  • Provide a professional opinion, legal finding, or government approval

The Evidence Receipt

Each reconciliation produces a signed Evidence Receipt — a portable artifact recording the declared outcome, the sources compared, and a cryptographic signature. The receipt can be independently verified by any reviewer using the Web Verifier without requiring the VaultBasis runtime. Verification confirms payload integrity and signature authenticity only; it does not establish that the underlying source data is correct.

Release Scope & Availability
